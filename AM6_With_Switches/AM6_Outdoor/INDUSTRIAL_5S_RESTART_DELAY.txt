AM6 Outdoor - Industrial 5 Second Circuit Restart Delay
========================================================

This build adds an independent NON-BLOCKING 5000 ms healthy qualification to
both Outdoor protection circuits.

Circuit A: SW1..SW4 -> R1/R3 -> statusout
Circuit B: SW5..SW8 -> R2/R4 -> statusoutB

STARTUP
-------
After a valid Indoor power=1 + outdoorEnable=1 request, each healthy circuit
starts its own 5000 ms timer. Its relay pair remains OFF during the timer.
The existing 1500 ms master re-arm guard runs inside that 5000 ms window; it
is not added on top. Therefore a continuously healthy circuit may energize
about 5 seconds after the valid Outdoor start request.

FAULT
-----
Any protection fault drops only the affected relay pair immediately and
resets only that circuit's timer.

FAULT RECOVERY
--------------
After all four protection inputs for that circuit become healthy again, that
circuit must remain continuously healthy for 5000 ms before its relay pair is
allowed ON again.

Circuit A and Circuit B qualify independently. No delay() blocking is used;
RS485, protection reads, temperatures and safety monitoring continue running.
