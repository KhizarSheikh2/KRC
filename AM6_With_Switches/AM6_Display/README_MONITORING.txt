AM6 DISPLAY - MONITORING ONLY
=============================

The JC2432W328 Display no longer controls AM6 power, fan speed or HVAC mode.
Those functions are controlled by physical switches on the Indoor master.

Display pages:
1. HOME
   - Power ON/OFF
   - COOL/HEAT
   - Fan LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH
   - Circuit A status + R1/R3
   - Circuit B status + R2/R4

2. OUTDOOR
   - Circuit A SW1..SW4 protection states
   - Circuit B SW5..SW8 protection states
   - R1..R4 states

3. ALERTS
   - statusout (Circuit A)
   - statusoutB (Circuit B)
   - Human-readable fault detail

Outdoor status values:
0 Stopped, 1 Running, 2 High PSI, 3 Low PSI, 4 Power Fault,
5 Overload/Tripped.

Theme: dark industrial, inversion OFF for the verified JC2432W328 panel.
