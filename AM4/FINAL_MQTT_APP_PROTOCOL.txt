AM4 FINAL MQTT / MOBILE APP PROTOCOL
====================================
Device name: AM4-AAA001

1) HARDWARE -> APP: TEMPERATURE CONFIGURATION
Topic: /KRC/AM4-AAA001/temperature_config_AM4

The value stored under each dynamic DS18B20 address is its assigned role:
  0 = unassigned
  1 = suction temperature
  2 = discharge temperature
  3 = supply temperature
  4 = return temperature
  5 = oil temperature
  6 = other sensor

IMPORTANT CORRECTIONS FROM THE PROVIDED EXAMPLES
------------------------------------------------
- The fifth sensor role is 5 (oil) and the sixth sensor role is 6 (Other Sensor).
- A comma is required after dischargelinetemp in telemetry JSON.
- JSON must not contain a trailing comma after the final property.
- oillinetempF is the Fahrenheit conversion of oillinetemp.
- othertempF is the Fahrenheit conversion of othertemp.
